# Tarnowskie Góry scene references

Researched 10 November 2026 against city, museum, railway, transport and heritage-register sources, and dated photographs. No reference material was supplied, so everything here was gathered online. This is a contemporary **compressed panorama**, not one street view: the south frontage of the Rynek is drawn in its real order, and the Evangelical church tower (north side of the same square), the miners' belfry (pl. Gwarków, about 300 m away) and the narrow-gauge train (its station is by the main railway station) are brought into the same picture. The season is summer, when the café gardens are out and the railway runs.

## The ordinary

- **Paving:** grey granite setts laid in overlapping fans, with straight pale slab lanes and low granite kerbs. The square was relaid in 2005 and 2010, and its east part rebuilt in 2020–2021.
- **Lamps:** black cast-iron posts with two scrolled arms, each carrying a hexagonal lantern with a small finial; warm light at night.
- **Furniture:** dark slatted benches, grey planters with a cross motif, tall conical flower towers in red, pink and white, bicycles leaning at racks, white and cream café parasols inside timber lattice fences.
- **Trees:** three small-leaved limes stand on the square (per the Rynek article); clipped globe-crowned trees stand in front of the town hall; birches line the narrow-gauge track in the operator's photographs.
- **Traffic:** the square is mostly on foot and by bicycle; a few cars and vans park at its edges. Regular ZTM buses are yellow and do not cross the square, so none is drawn.

## Composition recommendation

These are design suggestions in logical pixels, not geographical claims.

- **Skyline, left to right:** belfry around x = 15–49 with its roof apex near y = 54; church tower around x = 59–74 with the cross near y = 16; town hall from x = 96 to 192, turret spire near y = 8 at x ≈ 146; two plain tenements from x = 193 to 244, eaves near y = 72; Dom Sedlaczka from x = 246 to 304, roof ridge near y = 66; the arcaded house beyond a street gap from x = 311. The town hall is the tallest and the widest; a lime tree hides the seam between church and town hall.
- **Lettering:** `SEDLACZEK` in the 5-pixel font, 53 pixels wide, on the green wall between the floors at about x = 249–301, y = 100–106, with no lamp post in front of it. A small `500` jubilee board with the logo's four colours stands on the square at x = 64–84.
- **Ground:** building feet at y = 118; setts down to y = 129; a granite kerb at y = 130; then a grass verge and the narrow-gauge track, rails at y = 141 and 144, with meadow in front.
- **Life:** people on foot at y = 120–124, a cyclist just below them, pigeons near the kerb, a café garden with a waiter in front of the tenements, the train passing in both directions with its lamps and windows lit after dark. Both clocks show the hour. The square's lamps, the turret lantern, the clock faces, the café bulbs and the wine bar light up on separate schedules.
- **Sky:** the view looks south, so the sun stands over the roofs at noon, sets to the right behind Dom Sedlaczka and rises at the left behind the belfry.

## As built

The scene follows the layout above, with one change made after looking at the real switch: the track was first drawn along the bottom rows (rails at y = 146 and 149), where the frame's inner shadow hid the wheels and rails, so the kerb, the track and the walking lanes were each moved up five or six rows.

- The train is an Lxd2 and three coaches (closed, open summer car, closed), 188 pixels long and 16 tall, far shorter than a real train. It carries no fleet number. It crosses left to right and back on a 76-second cycle, runs until 23:00 and again from 7:30, and its roof (y = 128) stays two rows clear of the lowest walking lane and the cyclist. Having it cross the square is the collage; the real line does not.
- The pigeons leave before the train reaches them. A quick flick of the lens startles the cat on the belfry wall, lifts the pigeons and swings the bell; the bell otherwise hangs still, because when it is rung today was not found.
- Town hall windows go dark after office hours; only the council chamber and a few rooms stay lit into the evening. Floodlight on the town hall and church tower is painted in three steps from the foot upwards.
- The cyclist, the guided group with its pennant, the waiter, the swifts round the turret and the flag by the door are invented everyday figures, not referenced ones. White flagpoles stand before the entrance in the June 2024 photograph; the single pole here carries a Polish white and red flag.
- Clouds are flat-based summer cumulus; ground mist lies over the meadow around sunrise. Both are artistic choices.
- The tenements at Rynek 2 and 3 are drawn plainly, without shop names; the café garden in front of them stands for the several that fill the square in summer.

## Considered and not drawn

- **Historic Silver Mine and Black Trout Adit (UNESCO, 2017):** the attraction is underground. Its surface buildings were not photographed here in a form a resident would know by silhouette, so the mining story is carried by the belfry and the name of the square instead. The helmets belong underground and are not put on people in the square.
- **Steam-engine open-air museum:** its small green engine with bench cars is verified, but it runs inside the museum grounds two kilometres from the square. The public narrow-gauge railway is the better-known moving element.
- **The well, the miner sculpture (2015) and the ore-lump monument on the square:** read about, not seen in a photograph that was opened, so not drawn.

## Limits

No visit was made and no photograph was commissioned. The newest photographs opened of the town hall and Dom Sedlaczka are press images with file dates of September and October 2026; the rest are dated above. The church tower's and belfry's heights were not found, so their sizes against the town hall are a visual estimate. Night lighting colours and schedules are artistic, guided by the July 2021 night photograph. Whether jubilee boards with the 500 logo stand on the square was not verified; the logo and the anniversary are. The wine bar's opening hours differ between directories and were not confirmed with the venue. The UNESCO gallery returned HTTP 403 and was not opened.
